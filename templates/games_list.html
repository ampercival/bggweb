How recommendations work

Score = (3 × BGG average rating + player score) ÷ 4. The player score uses Best × 3 + Recommended × 2 − Not recommended × 2, then scales the catalog's range to 0–10. It stays consistent across filters, but may change after an import.

Playable means a raw player score of at least 150. Polls with fewer than 20 votes are marked Small sample; this is a visibility aid, not a statistical confidence estimate. Use the poll-vote minimum to choose your own evidence threshold.

Exact player-count filters use exact polls. Open-ended BGG buckets, such as 8+, keep their original labels and are shown separately. Rows with zero votes have no recommendation evidence.
